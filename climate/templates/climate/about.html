About the data

Every number on this site comes from the Met Office UK and regional series, published by the Met Office National Climate Information Centre. Each series is an average over a whole region, calculated from the 1 km HadUK-Grid dataset built from the UK land-surface station network. The values describe regions, not individual weather stations.

Reading the values

  • Winter spans two calendar years. Winter 2026 is December 2025 plus January and February 2026, so the first year of every series has no winter value.
  • The current year is incomplete. Months that haven't happened yet are simply absent, as are the autumn and annual values until the year ends. Recent months are provisional, and the Met Office may revise them.
  • A missing value is never shown as zero. A zero on this site is a real zero, such as a July with no air frost.
  • Seasonal and annual temperatures are day-weighted means of the months, and rainfall, sunshine and day counts are totals.
  • The trend is a straight-line fit (least squares) over the years you select, shown per decade. It summarises the direction of change and doesn't forecast anything.

How this site works

An ingest job downloads the {{ regions|length }} regional files for each measure from the Met Office, parses the fixed-width text and stores every value in PostgreSQL, updating values that the Met Office has revised. The explorer and compare pages draw their charts from this site's own public API, which you can use directly: 

curl "{{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}/api/v1/summary/?region=Scotland&parameter=Rainfall&period=ann"

The full list of endpoints, with a form to try each one, is in the API documentation.
